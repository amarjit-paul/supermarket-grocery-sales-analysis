# Supermarket Grocery Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes supermarket grocery sales data to understand overall business performance, product and category performance, profitability, discount behavior, and location-level performance.

The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, formulas, and statistical trend analysis.

## Business Objectives

The analysis answers key business questions such as:

- How is the overall grocery business performing?
- Which categories and products drive sales?
- Which products have high sales but comparatively lower profit margins?
- Is there a relationship between discount and profit?
- Which locations generate the highest sales?
- Which locations have high sales but comparatively lower profit margins?

##  Key Analysis

### Executive Performance
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Overall Profit Margin

- Regional sales performance

### Product & Category Performance
- Category-wise sales
- Subcategory sales
- High-sales products with comparatively lower profit margins

### Profitability & Discount Analysis
- Category-wise profit
- Region-wise profit
- Discount vs Average Profit per Order
- Linear trend analysis and R²

### Location Performance
- Top locations by sales
- High-sales locations with comparatively lower profit margins

##  Dashboards

The project contains four dashboards:

1. Executive Overview
2. Product & Category Performance
3. Profitability & Discount Analysis
4. Location Performance

##  Key Insights

- Sales and profit have a strong positive relationship at the location level.
- Some high-sales locations have comparatively lower profit margins.
- Soft Drinks is a high-sales product with a comparatively lower profit margin than other top-selling products.
- Discount shows virtually no linear relationship with average profit per order in the analyzed data.
